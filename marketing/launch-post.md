**Body:**

AI coding plans change their limits and prices often, usually without a changelog. I got tired of guessing what my plan included this week, so I built QuotaWatch.

- 35 plans across 8 tools: Claude, Cursor, GitHub Copilot, OpenAI Codex, Windsurf, Gemini CLI, Google Antigravity and the X Subscription.
- Each value quotes the vendor's own page (or official vendor post) and shows the date it was checked.
- Source pages are re-checked every 6 hours. A person reviews each change before it's logged. 7 changes are logged so far, for example:
  - Oct 12: Cursor moved into the X Subscription (Plus $8, Premium $30, Super $100, Ultra $200). Legacy Pro $20 / Pro+ $60 are closed to new customers.
  - Oct 15: Antigravity added Gemini 4 Argon for paid Google AI Pro, with a separate weekly-only quota.
  - Sep 14: Claude Code weekly limits settled at +25% over the pre-promo baseline.
- Includes a best-plan calculator, an RSS feed and the full dataset as JSON (CC BY 4.0).
- Free, with no signup and no affiliate links.

Prices are in USD as shown to a US visitor. If something's wrong or missing, tell me and I'll fix it.

https://quotawatch.vercel.app
