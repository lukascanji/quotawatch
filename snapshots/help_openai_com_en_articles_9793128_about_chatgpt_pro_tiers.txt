Updated: 28 minutes ago ChatGPT Pro now offers Pro 500, a new $500/month plan that includes GPT-6 Astra Ultrafast and GPT-6.1 Sol Ultrafast . Pro 200 is also available for new subscriptions again. New subscriptions that aren’t eligible for grandfathering include a lower usage allowance. Overview ChatGPT Pro includes Pro models, Codex, deep research, image creation, memory and file uploads. Pro plans Choose from three monthly Pro plans: Plan Monthly price (USD) Ultrafast Pro 100 $100 Not included Pro 200 $200 Not included Pro 500 $500 Included Pro 200 includes more usage than Pro 100. Pro 500 offers the highest included usage of the three plans. See the pricing page for a comparison of the features and usage included in each plan. Subscribe to a Pro plan Sign in to ChatGPT and open the pricing page to select a Pro plan. Use your existing ChatGPT account if you’re changing plans. Subscribe to Pro 200 again Yes. New Pro 200 subscriptions are available from the pricing page. New subscriptions that aren’t eligible for grandfathering include a lower usage allowance than previously offered with Pro 200 to reflect our increasingly efficient models. The monthly price remains $200. Changes to existing Pro 200 subscriptions Grandfathering applies if you had an active Pro 200 subscription at any point from September 22, 2026 through 10 a.m. Pacific Time on September 29, 2026. Eligible customers can use the previous included allowance through October 29, 2026 while their Pro 200 subscription is active. After that date, your subscription will move to the lower included usage allowance. Your subscription price stays at $200/month. Only users affected by this change will receive an email with additional details. Keeping your allowance and Pro 500 features No. Your subscription remains Pro 200. Keeping your current allowance does not upgrade your plan or add Ultrafast. Canceling and subscribing again If your Pro 200 subscription ended and you subscribe again on the same account, you qualify for grandfathering if you had an active Pro 200 subscription at any point from September 22, 2026 through 10 a.m. Pacific Time on September 29, 2026. You can use the previous included allowance through October 29, 2026 while your Pro 200 subscription is active. If you do not meet that eligibility window, your subscription uses the updated allowance. Scheduling a cancellation does not end your subscription immediately. You keep access until the end of the current billing period and can undo the cancellation before that period ends. If you believe your subscription ended because of an error, contact Support so we can review it. Usage and limits Pro plans with Ultrafast Among Pro plans, Ultrafast is available only on Pro 500. You can select it in the model picker. For GPT-6 Astra and GPT-6.1 Sol, it uses your plan’s included usage first at 8× the Standard rate for the selected model, then draws from your credit balance at 6× the Standard credit rate after that allowance is used. At launch, buying credits on Pro 100 or Pro 200 does not unlock Ultrafast. Temporarily unavailable models Model allowances vary by tier. At a model’s limit, it may be unavailable until its allowance resets. ChatGPT displays the reset time when available. A usage limit does not end your subscription or mean your account is restricted. Use another available model or wait for the reset. You cannot bypass a limit or increase it through a setting. Contact Support if the model remains unavailable after its allowance resets. Buying more usage For eligible features, you can purchase credits to continue after using your plan’s included allowance. Included usage is used first, followed by your credit balance. Credits support eligible usage in Codex, ChatGPT Work, ChatGPT for Word, ChatGPT for Excel and ChatGPT for PowerPoint. Availability depends on your plan and account. Credits do not provide additional usage for every ChatGPT model or feature. If credit purchases are available for your account, you can view your balance and purchase credits in Settings → Usage in ChatGPT, or Usage &amp; Billing in Codex. See Using Credits for Flexible Usage in ChatGPT (Personal plans) for supported features, rates and purchase details. Support and usage-limit resets Support cannot reset ChatGPT or Codex limits. We can investigate if usage was counted incorrectly or access failed to return after a reset. Account restrictions Pro use is subject to our Terms of Use . Sharing account credentials, reselling access and automatically extracting data are prohibited. We may restrict access for misuse. If you believe your account was restricted in error, contact Support to request a review. Different usage for similar tasks Usage depends on the model, the amount of context processed, reasoning, tool use, information retrieval, caching, and speed mode. Compared with Standard mode for the same model, Fast mode uses your included allowance at 2.5 times the rate, and Ultrafast for GPT-6 Astra and GPT-6.1 Sol uses it at 8 times the rate. These are allowance consumption rates, not guarantees of faster performance. When using purchased credits, Ultrafast is charged at 6× the Standard credit rate for the selected model. Managing your subscription Changing your Pro plan Open Settings → My Plan to view your subscription and available plan changes. Canceling your Pro subscription Cancel through the platform where you purchased your subscription: ChatGPT, Apple or Google Play. Canceling stops future renewals. You can normally keep using your plan until the end of the current billing period. Uninstalling the ChatGPT app does not cancel your subscription. See Canceling your ChatGPT subscription for the steps for each platform. Failed renewal payments A failed renewal payment does not by itself mean your subscription has ended. For web subscriptions, follow your billing email’s payment recovery instructions. For Apple or Google Play, check the store’s payment settings. Requesting a refund Refund eligibility depends on your purchase and applicable rights. Canceling your subscription does not automatically refund earlier charges. If a refund is approved, access covered by the refunded charge normally ends, subject to applicable law. For purchases on chatgpt.com or through Google Play, contact OpenAI Support while signed in to the account associated with the charge. For Apple App Store purchases, request a refund directly from Apple. See How do I request a refund for my ChatGPT subscription? for eligibility and instructions. Keeping Pro alongside a Business workspace Yes, if you keep your personal workspace separate. Your Pro subscription continues until you cancel it. Merging your personal workspace into Business changes how your subscription is handled; see Migrating your personal workspace to ChatGPT Business or Enterprise before merging. Pro billing frequency Pro is billed monthly. Annual billing and payment for multiple months in advance are not available. U.S. sales tax exemption requests Eligible organizations can submit exemption documents for review. See U.S. sales tax exemption requests for the required documentation and account details.
