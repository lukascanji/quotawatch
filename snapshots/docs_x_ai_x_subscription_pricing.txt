Free includes standard X. Paid individual plans include X Premium benefits when you connect your X account. New self-serve customers choose from The X Subscription plans. Existing Cursor, SuperGrok, and X Premium plans continue. Switching is optional. The X Subscription prices List prices for The X Subscription: Plan Monthly Annual Included usage Free $0 $0 Limited Plus $8/mo $80/yr ($7/mo) Base Premium $30/mo $300/yr ($25/mo) 4x Super $100/mo $1000/yr ($84/mo) 15x Ultra $200/mo $2000/yr ($167/mo) 50x For current prices, included usage, and terms, see x.ai/pricing . Grok Bot is included on Premium, Super, and Ultra. Plus does not include Grok Bot. Bot usage draws from that seat’s shared weekly usage pool, not a separate additive pool. Details: Grok Bot on The X Subscription and Usage limits . The shared included usage pool covers Grok Bot usage, and Grok models across Grok and Cursor. Top-ups buy Additional Usage credits for usage beyond what&#x27;s included. Third-party models in Cursor aren&#x27;t part of the included usage, so they use these credits from the first use. List prices do not include sales tax. Where required, tax may be added at checkout. The X Subscription for Teams Team plans are available on Premium at $30 per seat , Super at $100 per seat , and Ultra at $200 per seat . Each seat includes the same usage as the matching individual tier. Team members can also get X benefits from their seat when they connect their X account. Members of the same team can use different seat tiers, for example, three Premium seats and one Super seat. Team plans include centralized billing and administration, usage analytics, and the option to mix seat tiers. Plus isn&#x27;t available for teams. Existing plans Existing Cursor, SuperGrok, and X Premium plans continue unless you change or cancel them. See Do I have to switch? When you switch When you switch, eligible monthly and annual plans are cancelled, and you get a prorated refund for unused time. The refund goes back to your original payment method separately from your checkout total, so your new plan is charged at full price. Refunds can take a few days to appear, depending on your bank. The X Subscription starts immediately. If you pay for Cursor through the Apple App Store, switching doesn&#x27;t cancel that subscription, and we can&#x27;t refund it. Cancel it in your iPhone or iPad Settings so Apple stops billing you, and request any refund from Apple. Google Play treatment is covered separately in Google Play subscriptions .
