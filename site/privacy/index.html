Privacy Policy

Effective October 16, 2026

Who we are

QuotaWatch ("we") is a free, independent tracker of AI coding plan prices and limits, run by Lukas from Ontario, Canada. Contact: [contact email TBD].

What we collect

  • Server logs. Our host, Vercel, automatically processes standard request data such as IP address, user agent, requested URL and time, to serve and secure the site. We don't use these logs to build profiles.
  • Approximate location. If we add local-currency display, we may use an approximate country derived from your IP address or request headers. It is used in the moment and not stored by us.
  • Newsletter (when it opens). If you subscribe, we collect your email address. Subscriptions use double opt-in: you get a confirmation email, and you're only added once you click the link. Unconfirmed sign-ups are kept as unsubscribed and are not emailed again.
  • Sponsors. If you buy a sponsorship, we receive your business name, contact details and the payment status from Stripe. We never see or store full card numbers.

Cookies

QuotaWatch currently sets no cookies and uses no tracking or advertising cookies. If that ever changes (for example, privacy-friendly analytics), we will update this page first and ask for consent where required.

Third parties

  • Vercel: hosting and serverless functions.
  • GitHub: source code and automated data snapshots. No visitor data is stored there.
  • Resend: sending newsletter and confirmation emails and storing the subscriber list.
  • Stripe: processing sponsor payments.

Each provider handles data under its own privacy policy and may process it outside Canada.

How we use data

Only to run the site, send the emails you asked for, and handle sponsorships. We do not sell or rent personal information.

Retention and your choices

Every newsletter email has an unsubscribe link. You can ask us to access, correct or delete your data at [contact email TBD]. We delete subscriber data within 30 days of a verified request. Server logs are kept according to Vercel's retention settings.

Changes

We will post any changes here and update the effective date.
