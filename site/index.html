How often is QuotaWatch updated?

Every vendor source page is snapshotted every 6 hours. A detected change is reviewed by a person before it is published, with the date and an exact quote.

Is QuotaWatch affiliated with any vendor?

No. QuotaWatch is independent and uses no affiliate links.
