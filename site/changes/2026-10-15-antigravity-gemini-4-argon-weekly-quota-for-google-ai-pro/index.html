2026-10-15 · Google Antigravity · limit · official

Antigravity adds Gemini 4 Argon for paid Google AI Pro, with a separate weekly-only Argon quota

Before

Google AI Pro: one Gemini quota (Gemini 3.8 Flash), refreshed every 5 hours up to a weekly limit; Argon not yet available on Pro in Antigravity

After

Paid (non-trial) Google AI Pro also gets Gemini 4 Argon with its own weekly-only quota on top of the 3.8 Flash bucket; free Pro trials are excluded; Gemini 3.1 Pro, 3.6 Flash and 3.7 Flash retired outside Enterprise

Vendor quote

“We’re rolling out Gemini 4 Argon in Antigravity to paid Google AI Pro subscribers today. ... We’ve rolled out a separate weekly-only Argon quota for Pro on top of the existing Gemini bucket for 3.8 Flash”

“Available on Google AI Pro for non-trial subscriptions only.”
Official X post by @antigravity
