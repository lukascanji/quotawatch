Methodology

How QuotaWatch sources and checks its data.

  • Every value links to a vendor page (pricing page, docs or help center), with an exact quote and the date it was checked. Hover over a source link in the plans table to see the quote.
  • Confidence labels: official (vendor page), reported (press or staff post), observed (seen on a live page, change date unknown).
  • Every source page is snapshotted automatically every 6 hours. Any change in the text flags a human review before an entry is published.
  • Prices are in USD, before tax, unless noted. Limits are copied as vendors state them, and we don't invent numbers a vendor doesn't publish.
  • No affiliate links. Sponsors will be clearly labeled and never affect the data.
